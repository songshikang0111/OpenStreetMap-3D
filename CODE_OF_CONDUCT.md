# Contributor conduct

Everyone working on City Ink should be able to participate regardless of background or experience. Review the change, explain technical disagreements with examples, and respect contributors’ time and privacy.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language and sharing private information are grounds for moderation. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close a discussion, or limit participation. To request moderation, contact a repository maintainer privately through a contact method listed on their GitHub profile. Include the affected discussion and the action you are requesting.
